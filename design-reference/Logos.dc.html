CLEAR SPACE
Keep empty space around the mark at least the width of the D's wall on every side.
MINIMUM SIZE
Primary mark from 24 px. Below 24 px use the small version (bigger star, more gap).
COLOUR
The accent colour is used on the star only. The D is always ink, paper, black or white.
NEVER
No gradients, glow, shadows, outlines, rotation or stretching. Never move the star.
