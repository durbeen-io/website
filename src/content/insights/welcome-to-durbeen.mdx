AI is changing how we work, learn and build. Every week brings a new tool, a new model or a new promise. Most of it is noise. Some of it is genuinely useful. Durbeen is where I sort one from the other.

## What you'll find here

- **AI tools, tested first-hand.** I use the tools before I write about them, and I tell you what worked and what didn't.
- **Practical guides.** Step-by-step help for using AI in real work, in plain English.
- **Experiments.** Small tests with real results, not just opinions.
- **Infographics.** One idea per image, easy to save and share.
- **GitHub and open source.** Useful projects worth knowing about, explained simply.

## How I write

Everything here is tested first-hand and written plainly. AI helps me draft from my notes, and I review every word before it goes live. If I get something wrong, I'll correct it openly.

## Discover. Build. Innovate.

That's the idea behind Durbeen. Discover what's new, build something useful with it, and keep improving.

If that sounds useful, follow along. New articles are on the way.
